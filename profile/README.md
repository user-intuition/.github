# User Intuition

Qualitative research with real people, run from your AI agent or your code. Ask a question about your customers or shoppers; get AI-moderated interviews (typically 20–30 minutes, laddering 5–7 levels deep) and findings with verbatim quotes linked to each interview.

Participants come from a vetted 4M+ consumer panel or from your own customers, by voice, chat or video in 80+ languages. Paid launches stop for a human to approve the plan and the quote. [How quality is controlled](https://www.userintuition.ai/quality/) · [Pricing](https://www.userintuition.ai/pricing/)
